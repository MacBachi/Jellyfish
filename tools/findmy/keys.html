Find My keys for a jelly

Makes an OpenHaystack key pair here in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere: the page has no server and works offline. Keep the private key to yourself; the jelly only ever gets the public one.

Paste it into the OpenHaystack app (or macless-haystack, FindMy.py) as the accessory's private key.

"Send" posts it to http://192.168.4.1/api/findmy: you must be on the jelly's Wi-Fi and its provisioning window must be open (a button press within 60 s after power-up). Browsers hide the jelly's answer from this page, so check the Find My card on the jelly's own page afterwards. If sending is blocked (some browsers refuse it from a web-hosted page), copy the key and paste it into that card instead.
